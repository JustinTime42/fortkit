## What I got wrong, and what I did not do

- **My first draft of `bin/regent` would have kept xgul.7.1 red.** It kept the
  fallback to the retired path and explained itself by naming that path in
  comments. The guard is zero-tolerance on `bin/` and `fort/scripts/*.sh` — its
  historical-note exemption covers `fort/charter.md` only. I found this by
  reading the branch's linter before running it, not by running it, which is
  luck rather than method; the method is to read the verifier you are about to
  be judged by.
- **I wrote `__pycache__` into `bin/`** by running `python3 -m py_compile
  bin/civ-index` — probe residue in a gate-listed, kernel-RO directory. Removed
  before the commit. Use `python3 -c "compile(open(p).read(), p, 'exec')"`.
- **Two Mayor sessions started 2026-08-11 evening were live throughout and still
  carry the old instruction** (Marrek 20:21, Emrith 20:36). Launcher edits bind
  at dispatch. They were left running; nothing reaches them but a relaunch.
