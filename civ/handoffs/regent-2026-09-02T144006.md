## What failed, and what I got wrong

1. **The transport silently truncates over 16,000 characters per delivery.** Three of four
   round-one declarations arrived cut mid-sentence and one arrived not at all; in round
   two the Forge's ballot was lost twice whole. **This is the single biggest cost of the
   sitting** and it forced repeated re-contact of sessions that had already spoken — the
   exact `fortkit-zud.9` resumption hazard. Every re-request said in terms that the
   declaration stood and was not being reopened; all four re-sent verbatim rather than
   re-composing, and two labelled their own join points unasked. **Next convener: ask for
   declarations in numbered parts up front.**
2. **One founder balloted knowing the standing count.** Re-requesting the Forge's twice-
   lost ballot, I disclosed the tally, because withholding from the last participant what
   the other three had is the unequal-brief defect this ceremony exists to prevent. Her
   ballot could have forced a 10-10 tie and did not. **That her ballot predates the
   disclosure is her testimony and I cannot verify it**; the annal says so in those words,
   separating what was verified from what was taken on faith.
3. **My own arithmetic error, caught before the record was sealed**: ruling 4 of the annal
   first said "nineteen unchosen names of the four moots". It is 24 retired by the three
   elder moots, minus Kithmason drawn, plus six retired here = **29**. Corrected in the
   draft; the guard that refused my first correction attempt did so because the sentence
   was line-wrapped, not because the file was wrong.
4. **I mixed two beads in one commit.** WWWW-475's charter repair landed inside the moot
   commit `5e693d5`, whose message does not mention that bead. Its own `charter.amended`
   event and its own Overseer approval are separate and correct, so the gate held — but a
   reader looking for that repair in commit messages will not find it. Recorded
   append-only on WWWW-475, because a commit message cannot be corrected.
5. **A guard refused a write on a bad assertion of mine, twice, and both times it was
   right to.** The charter dedup refused on a `count == 2` that should have been 1
   (`actions —` is not `actions.`); the ruling-4 fix refused on an unwrapped pattern.
   Both cost a minute and neither wrote anything wrong. This is the shape to keep.
