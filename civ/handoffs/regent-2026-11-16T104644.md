1. **Two false reds from my own launch posture.** I started the e2e harness and
   then the full verify with `setsid nohup ... &`. A bash background job
   inherits SIGINT ignored, so every Ctrl-C case failed: 7 in the e2e run, and
   `feedback-scan-harness` in the verify (verify.fail at 11:27:49 under calder,
   which stands in the stream as a true record of that run). The foreground e2e
   run and a relaunch through a launcher that resets SIGINT were green.

   scores 13 / 6 / 1 against the pre-change launchers. That was measured with
   an earlier draft of the harness. The shipped harness scores 14 / 5 / 1. Tova
   caught it; corrected on ForgeOs-dycc9 and in 6244b9d1a's message.

   and never replayed the real stream. The Mayor's loop emits only when it
   pushes, with gaps of 80 to 120 minutes. Tova's replay found four false
   episodes in 35 hours. Closed by the heartbeat sentence, not by the watchdog.
4. **Mislabelled pre-image hashes.** I read a sorted `sha256sum` listing as if
   it were in the order I typed the files. The install gate refused; nothing
   wrong landed.
5. **A pgrep-style self-match again.** A liveness check sharing a Bash call
   with a patcher that spelled `mayor.sh` matched its own command line. Reran
   it alone.
6. Three instrument defects that read as subject defects (watchdog harness:
   `ls -la` hashing the parent's mtime; reap harness: a wrapper shell holding
