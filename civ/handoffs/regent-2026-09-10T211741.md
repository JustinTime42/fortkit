## What was built

`stall_check()`, called once per pass after `reap_pass` and `dispatch_pass`,
sharing the drain check's own answers: `live_forges()` once per pass,
`any_dispatchable()` at most once and only when nothing is live. A pass is a
stall when it **dispatched nothing AND reaped nothing AND no Forge is live AND
`any_dispatchable()` says work is startable**. `FLEET_STALL_PASSES=3`
consecutive stalls call `halt()` with a new `kind` argument of `stalled`:
`end_reason` is set explicitly on that path (`ForgeOs-6843`'s default is
never relied on and is untouched), `fleet.ended` carries reason
`stalled: …` plus a new `stalled:true` field, and the halt file and a
`FLEET STALLED` incident name the divergence — which bead was skipped after
`dispatchable()` said yes, and why (foreign `.forge.lock`, refused claim,
unreadable queue, no free slot). The counter resets on any pass that dispatched
or reaped, says so when it does, and the drain line reports its peak. A quiet
pass with a live Forge prints one `waiting:` line per quiet stretch saying why
the counter is not touched.

**The one departure from the bead's prose, and it is load-bearing.** The bead
lists three healthy outcomes (dispatch, reap, drain). The fourth — a pass that
does nothing because a Forge is live and the queue waits behind it — is every
quiet minute of the 14.2-minute-silence run the bead itself names as the thing
not to kill. The e2e harness generates the bead's literal three-term predicate
and shows it halting that healthy run (scenario 32b).

**The wedge recipe the bead names no longer wedges.** "bd ready clears it, the
dispatch guard refuses it" DRAINS on the shipped launcher since czhb. The
positive control uses the residual wedge `any_dispatchable()`'s own header
discloses: a startable bead whose worktree lock a process outside the fleet
holds. A tracker refusing every claim (`ForgeOs-dx34.2`'s contention shape) is
the second cause exercised.

**A stall is a `halt()`, not a plain exit.** Under the supervisor a stall that
ended without a halt file would be restarted into the same wedge every
N×FLEET_POLL seconds. Cost: a tracker outage longer than 90 s halts the night,
with the halt file saying why. Put on the bead for the Overseer to weigh at
signature; it is the one design call in the sitting a reasonable person could
take the other way.
