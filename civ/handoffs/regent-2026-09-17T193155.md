## What changed, and the design

**An approval is pinned to a CHANGE, not a bead.** The Keep's Approve now runs
`git rev-parse --verify --quiet bead/<suffix>` before any bd write
(`resolve-sha`, step 2 of seven), pins the tip in the comment
(` [approvedSha <sha> on bead/<sfx>]` or ` [approvedSha null; no bead/<sfx>]`)
and in the `gate.approved` payload (`-p '{"approvedSha","branch"}'`, additive;
the detail shape readers.mjs pins is unchanged). Exit 1 from git records null;
any other failure stops the signature at that step with no bd write. Decline
and Decide carry no pin and add no fleet-safe (bwfv's property, held by their
unchanged exact call lists). The allow list admits exactly the new shapes.

The fleet reads it in `dispatch_pass()` after `dispatchable()` said yes
(so `fleet-escalated`, `human`, non-open and open blockers refuse first,
criterion 4), after the launcher-lock skip and BEFORE the co-dispatch guard and
the budget (a landing is not a dispatch and spends no launch).
`signed_landing_ready()` asks three questions: the worker record stores the
verdict ESCALATE (`review_one()`'s ESCALATE arm now writes it); the newest
`gate.approved` for the bead in the last 14 day files carries an
`approvedSha`; the approved sha and the branch tip have the same
`git patch-id --stable` against their merge bases with main. All yes:
`signed_landing()` claims for the fleet actor, writes a fresh worker record,
emits one `fleet.signed` naming sha and patch-id, runs `prelanding_refresh()`,
RECOMPUTES the patch-id on the refreshed tip (a clean refresh that changed it
routes to verifier + `review_one()`; a conflict is paged as before), runs the
host verifier, and lands a green tree through `land()` on the fleet's own token
`OVERSEER-SIGNED` (admitted by `verdict_is_landable()`, files no follow-up,
closes like APPROVE; the merge message and close reason name the approval and
the stored ESCALATE). A red verifier on the SIGNED change is PAGED, never
returned. "Same change" is a patch-id and not a sha because the pre-landing
refresh moves the tip on every landing.

**Rider B.** `escalate_labels()` beside `page_overseer()` does the
`fleet-safe`→`fleet-escalated` write, captures `label_rc`, sets `label_note`;
the four post-merge sites put that sentence where they used to assert the
labels. The ESCALATE arm captures its own write's exit; on failure:
needs-person, an `incident` naming `bd_exit` with `returned:false`, and NO
`fleet.returned`. Audit: 16 `bd … || true` sites before, 5 changed, 11 remain
with reasons on the bead; the one worth a bead is `--add-label fleet-return-N`
in `return_to_ready()` (:823), the return counter itself.
