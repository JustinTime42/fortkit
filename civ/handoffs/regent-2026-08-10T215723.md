## What changed (commit 2e0744d, both copies)

`fort/scripts/warden.sh` (live) and `templates/fort/scripts/warden.sh` (blank).
fortkit's warden.sh was well behind longburn's; two of the three fixes are
backports of longburn 5if/8ur (node_modules) and qe2 (bd export), the third is
NEW (neither fort had it).

1. **DEFECT 1 — silent candidate-dir default to main. FIXED (new work).**
   `src` no longer defaults. The launcher parses the ref-range tip
   (`tip="${range##*..}"`, handles `A..B`/`A...B`/bare ref), and when arg 3 is
   omitted DERIVES the candidate dir from `git worktree list --porcelain` (the
   worktree whose HEAD == the tip commit), or main if the tip is already merged.
   In EVERY case it then asserts the tip is reachable from `$src`'s HEAD and
   REFUSES `exit 68` otherwise — so a *wrong explicit* arg 3 is caught too, not
   just the omitted case.

2. **DEFECT 2 — verifier re-runs impossible. FIXED, and it needed MORE than the
   longburn pattern.** node_modules stays rsync-excluded (219MB) and is bound
   read-only through the mask (lockfile-guarded, `npm ci --offline` fallback on
   mismatch). **longburn tmpfs's only `node_modules/.vite`; measured here that
   is INSUFFICIENT for fortkit** — vitest bundles the TypeScript
   `vitest.config.ts` to `node_modules/.vite-temp` and died EROFS there, so
   tsc+biome ran but every test was TAKEN ON FAITH (the smoke reproduced the
   bead's exact complaint on the first pass). Adding a second tmpfs over
   `node_modules/.vite-temp` fixed it: full suite (14 files, 152 tests) runs in
   the exact Warden mask.

3. **DEFECT 3 — bd unavailable in-mask. FIXED (backport of qe2).** Fresh
   `bd export` seeded host-side to `$scratch/.beads-export.jsonl` each launch;
   briefing directs the seat to it and states it may lag while the injected bead
   text is authoritative. Confirmed the failure mode: bd in-mask returns
   `openat LOCK: read-only file system`.

   The briefing's "safe for build/test re-runs" claim is now TRUE, backed by a
   VERIFIER RECIPE (gate = `CI=1 fort/scripts/verify.sh --no-emit`; allowed
   direct legs `npx tsc` / `npx vitest run`; biome only via verify.sh in this
   profile) and a BEADS ACCESS block. Smoke prompt upgraded to probes 9/10/11 so
   every future launch self-checks all three defects.
