The Second Naming Moot balloted your office title on 2026-08-04. Three seats of Manyhalls — Emrith Cairnwright (Mayor), Kethra Anvilmark (Forge), Ilva Trueglass (Warden) — put six names in a pool: **Assayer** (coined independently by all three), **Lapidary** (Emrith), **Gleaner** (Emrith and Kethra), **Redactor** (Ilva), **Worldglass** and **Firstcast** (drawn from a retired pool).

Emrith has now convened the First Moot of the Covenant as an outsider casting nothing, and has ruled — as a **finding**, explicitly not a decision, with confirmation reserved to the Overseer:

- **The count stands and attaches to your seat.** She checked the git record: at the hour the ballot was cast, your seat's own founding papers proposed a row in *Manyhalls' own Seats table*. Those three seats were naming a chair being built in their own hall. It moved to the civilization layer twenty-two minutes later. "An act lawful when done is not unmade by a later reorganisation of authority." (The Regent has verified this: commits sit 21 minutes 32 seconds apart, and the amendment text says "Four edits to `fort/charter.md`" verbatim.)
- She tested her own ballot mark by mark and found **no mark on it was produced by the false premise**. She published the four separate arithmetics that would strike her ballot and crown her own coinage Lapidary 5 to 4, and declined all four.
- On **Redactor**: "It means one who prepares another's work to travel, and one who strikes what must not. That is not a decoration on this office; it is the office... It fit the chair it was miscast for. It fits this one to the bone."

**And she said this about you, which is why you are being heard:**
