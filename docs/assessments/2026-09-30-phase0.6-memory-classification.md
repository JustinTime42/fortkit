## Method

- `civ/remember.md` (5,121 lines, 505 entries) split at entry boundaries into four
  ranges (1-1720, 1721-2757, 2758-3824, 3825-5121), one agent each.
- Facts ledgers: Proofdelve (ForgeOs, 101 files) one agent; Manyhalls (14),
  Farlantern (longburn, 32) and Kithmason (WWWW, 4) one agent.
- Rubric: **SCAR** (a procedural rule about how not to hold a tool; name the cheapest
  mechanical control), **WHY** (intent, decision, doctrine), **RESTATES-CODE** (what a
  code index could answer), **RECORD** (history, never injected). One kind per entry;
  mixed entries take the dominant kind and are marked.
- Row counts in the appendices match the sources exactly (179 + 90 + 90 + 146 = 505
  entries; 101 and 50 fact files).

**Limits.** Classification is model judgement, one pass, no second rater. Recurrence
counts are what the text itself records ("third time", "sixth entry") or a later entry in
the same chunk; a recurrence recorded in a different chunk is joined below by the Mayor by
reading the gists, not by a mechanical key, so the merged families are approximate.

## What this decides

1. **Phase 3.3 scope.** Controls 2, 3, 7, 9, 10 and 12 are one PreToolUse hook with a rule
   table: a fleet-able module (rules plus tests against real command lines) behind a
   Regent-cut seam in the hooks config. Control 4 is one lint; control 8 lands inside the
   Phase 1.3 adapter; control 1 is the Regent's own close-out and wake. Families 5, 6 and
   11 are conventions, not hooks.
2. **Proof obligation.** Each hook rule ships with a positive control (the scar's own
   recorded command line must be refused) and a vacuity control (a benign neighbour must
   pass), per the spine's fourth rule.
3. **`fortkit-nqoy` (civ/remember.md to the ledger).** The appendices are the migration
   worksheet: WHY entries become facts with anchors (Phase 3.2), RESTATES-CODE entries are
   deleted once the index answers them, RECORD entries stay history, and SCAR entries
   become a control or a fact that names its control.
4. **The briefing.** Of the 551 KB the Regent is handed at wake, the part that is doctrine
   (WHY) is about a third of `remember.md`'s entries. The rest is either a control that
   does not yet exist, a restatement a lookup would answer, or history.
