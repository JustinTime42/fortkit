# Charter amendment: the fifth seat (fortkit-zud.1)

Drafted by the Regent under edict, 2026-08-04. Applied by the Overseer's hand
under gate 1. Four edits to `fort/charter.md`.

The Regent could have applied these directly — the charter is writable to the
Regent and read-only to every seat. It did not, and the reason belongs on the
record: gate 1 exists so that no agent seats itself. An edict that founds a seat
by rewriting the constitution in the same motion would hollow out the gate while
formally honouring it. So these are proposed exactly as Emrith proposed the
Herald's, and the Overseer applies them.

## Rationale (records the need, per the amendment rule)

Need-driven, not failure-driven, like the Herald's. The civilization has
produced real, measured security findings as a by-product of ordinary work —
several of them about the agent-harness tooling itself, and generalizable well
past this machine. Nothing exists to carry them outward at a standard that would
survive a stranger's scrutiny, and the Overseer does not want to spend his own
hours on it.

The seat is additive and narrowing: no gate is weakened, gate 3 is stated more
strongly than before, the seat's writes stay outside every fort's product tree,
and it holds no capability to reach the outside world. The one genuine expansion
is read access across all three forts plus the ability to execute reproductions,
which is the minimum the craft requires and is the reason this is a separate seat
from the Herald rather than a second lane of hers.
