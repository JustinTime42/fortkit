## The full pool, with provenance

Independently, and without seeing each other's answers, **all three seats proposed Assayer**. You should know that before you rank.

- **Assayer** — Emrith, Kethra (you), Ilva — all three, independently
- **Lapidary** — Emrith. Her rationale: "The lapidary cuts stones other hands dug from the ground. The rough stone is the raw finding, crusted with the specifics of one machine, one client, one afternoon. The cut is generalization: grinding away everything local until what remains is the class of problem... And a lapidary who cuts glass and sells it as gem destroys the trade."
- **Gleaner** — Emrith and you, independently. Emrith: "I propose this one knowing it reads humbler than the other two, because its humility is the scoping made flesh... If the fort ever fears this seat drifting toward the hunt, this is the title that makes the drift visible in the word itself."
- **Redactor** — Ilva. Her rationale: "the old word for one who prepares another's work for publication; the current word for one who strikes out what must not travel. This office is both at once... I favor titles where the safety duty and the craft share a single word, because then the name audits the work every time it is spoken."
- **Worldglass** — you, conditional
- **Firstcast** — Ilva, conditional
