## The full pool, with provenance

Independently, and without seeing each other's answers, **all three seats proposed Assayer**. You should know that before you rank.

- **Assayer** — Emrith, Kethra, Ilva (you) — all three, independently
- **Lapidary** — Emrith. Her rationale: "The lapidary cuts stones other hands dug from the ground. The rough stone is the raw finding, crusted with the specifics of one machine, one client, one afternoon. The cut is generalization: grinding away everything local until what remains is the class of problem... And a lapidary who cuts glass and sells it as gem destroys the trade."
- **Gleaner** — Emrith and Kethra, independently. Kethra: "Gleaning is by law and custom what happens after the harvest, never instead of it. A gleaner who plants in order to have something to gather has stopped being a gleaner." Emrith: "If the fort ever fears this seat drifting toward the hunt, this is the title that makes the drift visible in the word itself."

## The convener's rulings so far

Emrith ruled before any ballot that **only the office title is balloted**; the occupant declares their own given name, family word, pronouns and charge without leave. The Herald's chair is empty and casts no ballot — absence, not abstention. Three ballots: Forge, Warden, Mayor. She proposed Assayer, Lapidary and Gleaner and **withdrew all three from her own ballot** per the Splitstone precedent.

## What the Forge said that touches your ballot

Kethra **struck Firstcast** as standing on her own ground, by ruling 2, which follows the word and not the proposer: "read as casting metal, it stands inside the Forge's ground, my ground, and by ruling 2 that conflict follows the word no matter who proposes it." Note that under the founding practice conflicts are *recorded, not acted* — the pool stood whole at nine last time — so Firstcast is not struck from the pool by her objection, only weighted by it.

Kethra also declared a conflict against her own proposal and **will withhold her vote on Assayer**: "an assayer strikes a mark into what passes the test, and I took the surname Anvilmark at the last moot... If the name is sound, it will not need my point to carry."

## Evidence verified from the record by the Regent, since you spoke

- **"Assayhold" appears 12 times** across the civilization's records — the precedent name in Proofdelve's ruling of record, the one Manyhalls' ruling 2 widened.
- **"Marrowassay" appears 8 times** — the family word of **Tova Marrowassay, Warden of Proofdelve**, a living citizen of another settlement, and a fellow holder of your own office in another fort.
- Against that: the charter holds **"New fort, new founders — other settlements' citizens remain their own."**

That last point lands closest to you: the *assay* root is carried by another fort's Warden. You are the seat that changed her own given name rather than ask a colleague to change hers, on the reasoning that the seat which intends to flag a defect class in other forts' data should eat the fix in its own.
