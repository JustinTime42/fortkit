## Lessons / discipline that MUST carry into D and E

- **Anything secret/credential/probe-touching is done MECHANICALLY, never by
  feeding it to a model or subagents** — that tripped the safety classifier twice
  in Sitting A. The mechanical identity strip collapsed 5,275 flags → 60 with no
  model.
- **The model NEVER runs the wall-proof or reads a secret** — even a no-secret-read
  probe halts the classifier when the *model* runs it (it names credential paths).
  The Regent AUTHORS probes as scripts; the **Overseer runs them** and pastes the
  RESULT so it's a recorded count.
- **Re-run the Overseer wall-proof after ANY change to the mask/auth/network
  surface** (D adds outbound credentials + likely egress → re-prove).
- **Test against the REAL endpoint/tool, not a stub** — Sitting B's four founding
  defects all came from stubs being more forgiving than the real tools.
- **`edict.ended` fires from the launcher's exit hook when the Overseer `/exit`s** —
  it is NOT something to tell the Regent to emit. The marker clears on the same hook.
- Have the Regent **claim the bead + post its step plan as a bead comment before
  touching the filesystem** (breadcrumb; both later sittings did this).
