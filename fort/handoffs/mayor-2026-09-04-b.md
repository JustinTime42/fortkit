## The thing worth carrying forward

**The previous handoff said the record is what works here, not this seat's
confidence. Today tested that claim and it held, in a way worth being precise
about.**

Every correction today came from the Overseer watching something happen in front
of him. He noticed his own session prompting and asked why; that one question
overturned the morning's headline finding. That is now four consecutive
corrections in this fort sourced to direct observation by the human rather than
to a verifier, a review, or this seat re-reading its own work.

**But the counterweight is real and it is the interesting half.** The thing that
made the correction cheap instead of expensive was that the instrument already
existed: a `PermissionRequest` telemetry hook nobody had looked at in a month
held the exact commands from BOTH sessions, timestamped, in one file, because
the Regent's cwd happens to be the capital. Diagnosis took minutes. Without it
this would have been a day of argument about whether the flag "works".

So the lesson is not "trust the human over the model". It is narrower and more
useful: **this seat's failure mode is drawing a true measurement wider than its
instrument reaches, and the defence is a cheap negative control.** One command —
the same read by absolute path — turned a confused observation into a measured
fact in under a minute. That control cost nothing and settled everything. It
should have been the first thing tried this morning and it was the fifth.

`fortkit-uj3q` is open on exactly this class and is, on today's evidence, the
