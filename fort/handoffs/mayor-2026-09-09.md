## 8. THE THING WORTH CARRYING FORWARD

**The dominant defect class in this civilization right now is controls that do not
discriminate, and it was named, split and mechanised inside two days.**
`ForgeOs-8zb7`. Marrek's three classes:

| | mechanism | cost |
|---|---|---|
| **A. cannot fail** | failure branch unreachable | fails silent, false negative |
| **B. fails for the wrong reason** | pins a spelling | fails loud and wrong |
| **C. cannot run, blames its subject** | instrument breaks | misattributes |

C later split into **C-loud** (blames the subject) and **C-quiet** (**certifies**
it), and C-quiet is strictly worse because a green score is the outcome nobody
investigates.

**The anchor is three-outcome discrimination:** red against the defect, **green
against a correct variant differing only in spelling**, and a distinct
"I could not run" outcome. Step 2 had never been asked for anywhere in that fort.
It has now been performed, and Tova's ruling on it is the keeper:

> **a step-2 result that cannot be re-executed is a claim rather than a control,
> which is the exact distinction `8zb7` exists to enforce.**

Instances are past a dozen and include several of Marrek's, several of the
Regent's, and at least two of mine. **The taxonomy started catching things at the
point of writing rather than at review within hours of existing**: a magic number
refused, an inert glob guard found, a harness that certified a regression.

**And the second thing, which is the one the Overseer should hold:** on `4k3`,
Marrek wrote an acceptance criterion as a *measurement* naming "change nothing"
as a legitimate outcome, rather than asserting the answer he believed. His belief
was wrong. The builder measured, found the opposite, took the other branch, and
reported it. **Had it been written as "confirm X and close the item," his error
would have entered the trunk behind a green verifier and an approving review.**

> **Acceptance criteria are the load-bearing control in unattended work, because
> they are the only place a human's intent survives into a room with no human in
> it. Criteria that assert a conclusion convert a Mayor's mistakes into merged
> code. Criteria that demand a measurement catch them.**

That is what the Overseer is actually buying when he spends an evening on bead
text instead of on the fleet.
